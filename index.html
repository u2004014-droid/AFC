Research interests

I am interested in how language and multimodal models behave when conditions are hard, and in making them dependable there.

Reliability and security of language models

How models get misled, jailbroken, or manipulated by what they read, and which defenses hold up against an attacker who knows about them. This is a newer area for me, and I am coming to it from LLM fine-tuning, multi-agent systems, and adversarial evaluation.

Multimodal understanding

Models that combine text, images, and speech, especially on noisy or misleading content such as memes and fake news.

AI for healthcare

Medical imaging when labeled data is scarce, and explainable models for clinical prediction.

Low-resource languages and speech

How well general-purpose LLMs and speech models carry over to Bengali and Arabic, and what it takes to close the gap.

Current work

Few-shot SAM for echocardiography

Adapting the Segment Anything Model to segment the four heart chambers in blurry, grainy ultrasound when only a few labeled frames exist. The method perturbs prompts in polar coordinates around the probe apex to respect the fan shape of the image. Early results on EchoCP, EchoNet-Dynamic, and CAMUS are promising, and the manuscript is in preparation.
